FAQs for kitchen professionals

When your client has a particular look in mind

My client has seen a particular stone pattern. Can you find something similar?

Often, yes. Send the image or bring it into the showroom. With natural stone such as granite, marble & quartzite, every slab is naturally different, so we would not promise an exact copy. With quartz & porcelain, named manufactured designs are generally more repeatable, although availability and variation can still depend on the product.

Can you match the exact Taj Mahal quartzite my client has seen online?

We can look for Taj Mahal quartzite with the character your client likes, but natural quartzite varies from slab to slab. Colour, veining & movement will not be identical to somebody else’s kitchen. Where appearance is central to the design, we would rather help you look at the actual material available than promise an exact online match.

Are porcelain worktops more consistent than natural stone?

Generally, yes. Porcelain is manufactured, so the visible design is more repeatable than a naturally occurring slab of marble, granite or quartzite. We would still check the particular product being specified rather than make a blanket promise across every range or batch.

Can my client choose the actual natural stone?

Where exact movement, colour or veining matters, talk to us about the project. Natural stone should be treated as an individual material. This becomes especially important on statement islands, larger splashbacks & designs where the stone is one of the main visual features.

What if the exact stone my client wants is not available?

Show us what they like about it. We can look at suitable quartz, granite, marble, quartzite, porcelain or Dekton options with a similar direction.

Can I bring my client to the showroom?

Yes. That is often useful when the client needs to compare materials, veining, pattern scale & overall colour in person before making the final decision.

Do you template, fabricate & fit?

Yes. RH Stone Surfaces can handle the worktop process from templating through fabrication to installation based on the agreed project details.
