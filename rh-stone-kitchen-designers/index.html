When the standard choice is not enough

Bring us the awkward, individual or very specific part of the design.

A large island with strong movement. A client who has saved one particular stone image for months. A splashback that needs to feel connected to the worktop. An unusual sink or hob arrangement. A kitchen where the stone becomes one of the main visual features.

You do not need to know the exact product before contacting us. If the client has a Pinterest image, Instagram kitchen, sample or photograph of a slab they love, bring it. We can look at what they actually like about it: colour, movement, veining, warmth, contrast or scale.

FAQs for designers & trade customers

Questions before the stone is signed off

My client has seen a particular stone pattern. Can you find something similar?

Often, yes. Send the image or bring it into the showroom and we can look at the colour, movement & overall character they are trying to achieve. Natural granite, marble & quartzite vary slab to slab, so we would not promise an exact copy of somebody else’s kitchen.

Can you match exact Taj Mahal quartzite from an online photo?

We can look for Taj Mahal quartzite with the character your client likes, but natural quartzite varies from slab to slab. If slab appearance is central to the design, seeing actual available material is the better route.

Can my client visit the South Shields showroom?

Yes. Bring the client, kitchen plans, cabinet samples, flooring samples or inspiration images and compare suitable materials in person.

Do you template, fabricate & fit?

Yes. RH Stone Surfaces can handle the worktop process from templating through fabrication to installation.
