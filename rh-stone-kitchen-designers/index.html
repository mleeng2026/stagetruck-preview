Stone Worktops for Kitchen Designers, Studios & Trade

RH Stone Surfaces supplies, templates, fabricates & installs premium stone worktops and surfaces for kitchen designers, kitchen studios, kitchen companies & trade professionals across Newcastle, Durham, Sunderland & the North East.

For Kitchen Designers, Studios & Trade

You’ve designed the kitchen. We help you get the surface detail right.

Sometimes a straightforward quartz is exactly right. Other times the client wants something more individual: Taj Mahal quartzite, natural marble, distinctive granite, a Calacatta-style quartz or porcelain, or a surface that becomes one of the main features of the room.

Bring us the kitchen plan, inspiration images, cabinet sample, flooring sample or photos. We can help you work through the surface choice, templating, fabrication & installation.

Bring us the awkward, individual or very specific part

Large islands, full-height splashbacks, waterfall ends, sink runs, stone shelves, vanity tops, utility surfaces and carefully positioned natural veining can all be discussed from the plan.
