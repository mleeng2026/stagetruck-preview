FAQs for designers & trade customers

Questions your client may ask you before the stone is signed off

My client has seen a particular stone pattern. Can you find something similar?

Often, yes. Send the image or bring it into the showroom and we can look at the colour, movement & overall character they are trying to achieve. Natural granite, marble & quartzite vary slab to slab, so we would not promise an exact copy of somebody else’s kitchen.

Can you match the exact Taj Mahal quartzite my client has seen online?

We can look for Taj Mahal quartzite with the character your client likes, but natural quartzite varies from slab to slab. Colour, veining & movement will not be identical to another installation. If the slab appearance is central to the design, seeing actual available material is the better route.

Are porcelain & quartz more consistent than natural stone?

Generally, yes. They are manufactured surfaces, so named designs are usually more repeatable than natural stone. We would still check the particular product, batch & availability rather than promise a perfect match.

Can my client choose the actual natural stone?

Where colour, veining or movement is central to the design, talk to us about the project. This is especially relevant for statement islands and large splashbacks where the stone becomes a major visual feature.

What if the exact material in the inspiration image is not available?

Show us what the client likes about it. We can look at suitable quartz, granite, marble, quartzite, porcelain or Dekton with a similar direction rather than simply stopping at “that one is unavailable”.

Can I bring my client to the South Shields showroom?

Yes. That is often the best way to compare colour, movement, pattern scale & overall feel before the final decision.

Do you template, fabricate & fit?

Yes. RH Stone Surfaces can handle the worktop process from templating through fabrication to installation, based on the agreed project details.
